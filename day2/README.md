# Day 2 — Vectors (Word Embeddings)

From counting words to representing meaning. The distributional hypothesis, how embeddings
are trained, how to use them, and what they reveal (and absorb) about society.

## Slides
1. **Embeddings Foundations** — the distributional hypothesis, meaning as geometry
2. **Embeddings in Practice** — Word2Vec, GloVe, similarity, visualization
3. **Bias & Measurement** — embeddings as a lens on society, validation, the bridge to context
4. **Bonus: fastText & Embeddings as Features** — subwords/OOV, and embeddings for classification

## Notebooks
1. **BoW Approaches** — dictionary, supervised ML, and topic modeling on real data
2. **BoW Limits** — hands-on demonstrations of where bag-of-words breaks
3. **Embeddings** — pre-trained GloVe, similarity, analogies, visualization, training your own, bias
4. **Bonus: Embeddings as Features** — comparing embeddings vs. TF-IDF for classification

Each notebook comes with an `_exercises` version and a matching `_solutions` version.

Runs on a normal CPU runtime.
